## What is live (all tests green on prod 2026-10-29: 26/26 API, 15/15 UI, 21/21 unit)

- **Engine.** The planner (Score) picks a teaching pattern per lesson. The lesson writer (Stage) writes recipe JSON. The checker (`animation/validate.ts`) replays every step and allows one repair turn. Our renderers draw it.
- **Lesson format.** Each step is `say`, `callouts`, `set`/`focus`/`play`, a `task` (slide, tap or predict) and `after`. A quiet "Show me" skips the task. Affordances: pulsing handles, direction arrows, tap rings, glowing knob thumbs.
- **Prompts.**
  - `src/lib/animation/guide.ts`: the visual teaching guide, subject by subject.
  - `src/lib/animation/playbook.ts`: 17 hand-written, validated example lessons.
  - Icons: `src/lib/icons.ts` (130 Lucide icons, generated by `scripts/build-icons.mjs`).
  - Learning-science basis: `docs/research/2026-10-28-learning-science.md`.
- **Rules held in code.**
  - Greetings and filler never become courses (`intent.ts`).
  - The learner's language is detected and forced through every stage.
  - Lesson 1 is never a diagram, and there is at most one per course.
- **Visuals.** 2D has shadows, gradients, shaded balls, glow, draw-in, a dot grid and content-aware framing. 3D has a studio-lit parts library. Motion uses springs.
- **Models.** Chinese only: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, then GLM 5.3 Flash, then MiniMax M3.1 Flash. Calls are hedged. No thinking: with the 18k-token prompt, DeepSeek thinking timed out at 80 s.
- **Cost (measured on prod).** About $0.002 per plan (Parallel web search, $0.001) and about $0.002 per lesson. Repeats are served from the Vercel Runtime Cache for free. Soft cap: `DAILY_CAP_USD`=10.
- **Library.** `src/lib/library.ts` holds the curated courses; one, "How AI works", is hand-authored from playbook lessons. Building more is parked by Logan.

## Learned the hard way

- A slideshow (picture, sliders, paragraph) is not Brilliant. The learner must act inside the picture on every step (Logan: 2/10).
- Prompt-only rules get ignored by cheap models without thinking (flowcharts, language). Enforce the important ones in code.
- Thinking on DeepSeek with a big prompt is too slow; quality has to come from the guide, the playbook and the checker.
- `useSize` needs a callback ref when the element mounts later; otherwise the overlays covered the scene.
- Length clipping can cut `{...}` templates in half, so templates are clipped safely and broken holes never render.
- Next dev runs one server per directory; for A/B runs, restart with `LESSON_THINK=off`/`on`.
